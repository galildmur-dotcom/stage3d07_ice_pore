## Scientific observations

- From M1 to M5 derivative decreases monotonically by 0.26468505784 m⁻¹ (~10.07% relative to M1). Thereafter it oscillates around ~2.37 m⁻¹. The narrow range M5–M11 is approximately 2.36133–2.37694 m⁻¹, span 0.01561 m⁻¹ (~0.66% of 2.37). A narrow global band is not an error bound.
- Absolute consecutive *global* changes are not monotone: M9→M10 0.00022704 m⁻¹ followed by M10→M11 0.00388398 m⁻¹. For these last two pairs, the matched full signed 4D-bin L1 decreases 0.80799039→0.72225897 m⁻¹. Thus a tiny signed sum (M9→M10 cancellation factor ~3559×) hides significant spatial/time changes.
- A complete comparable 4D L1 series currently covers M2→M3 through M10→M11: 4.95885, 2.66935, 2.13647, 1.92912, 1.06603, 0.95393, 0.96421, 0.80799, 0.72226 m⁻¹. The M1→M2 published L1 4.381308 is **spatially aggregated after time summation**, not the same 4D norm and is intentionally omitted from this plot. M6→M7 through M10→M11 L2 is 0.02337383, 0.01946623, 0.02089089, 0.01510925, 0.01167642 m⁻¹. Early comparable 4D L2 values are not verified in these selected reports and are intentionally omitted.
- The full 4D difference L1 mostly shrinks with adaptation, but M8→M9 increases slightly relative to M7→M8; the recent reduction does not prove an asymptotic rate. The consecutive correction vectors have negative correlations in the late sequence. No Richardson extrapolation or rigorous error bar should be claimed.
- To better diagnose future convergence, complement these figures with the 4D L1/L2 by radial shell, polar vs nonpolar groups, and late-time block; signed separate geometry/stress and equilibrium/history contributions; polar curved 729QP effective volume (actual independent h-like resolution). Graph against total Tet10 count is informative but not a valid uniform-h convergence rate because adaptation is nonuniform.
